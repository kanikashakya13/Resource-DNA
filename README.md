# 🧬 Resource DNA
### The Building Blocks of Every Breakthrough.

**Resource DNA** is a scientific resource-sharing platform designed to connect researchers, students, laboratories, and equipment providers in one collaborative ecosystem.

From individual scientific instruments to fully equipped research laboratories, Resource DNA aims to make research resources more accessible through resource discovery, booking management, availability tracking, QR-based handovers, and delivery coordination.

---

## 🌟 About the Project

Every great discovery begins with the right resources.

Resource DNA helps bridge the gap between people who need scientific resources and those who have them. The platform enables researchers and students to discover laboratory equipment and research facilities, request bookings, and coordinate resource handovers.

Providers can manage their resources, update availability, and handle booking requests, while Delivery Partners can coordinate assigned deliveries.

Our vision is to encourage collaboration, improve resource accessibility, and help researchers focus on experimentation, exploration, and innovation.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔐 **User Authentication:** Account registration and login with role-based access.
- 👥 **Role-Based Dashboards:** Separate experiences for Researchers, Providers, and Delivery Partners.
- 🔬 **Resource Discovery:** Browse laboratories and scientific equipment.
- 🏢 **Laboratory & Equipment Management:** Providers can manage resource listings and availability.
- 📅 **Booking Management:** Researchers can request bookings, while Providers can approve or reject requests.
- ⚙️ **Availability Tracking:** Manage resource statuses and identify resources that are available, booked, under maintenance, or reserved for demonstrations, where supported.
- 📱 **QR-Based Handover and Return:** Support resource handovers and returns through booking-specific QR verification.
- 🚚 **Delivery Coordination:** Manage assigned deliveries and display relevant handover or return QR codes.
- 🔔 **Notifications:** Communicate relevant booking, handover, return, and delivery updates.
- 🪪 **Identity Verification:** Support identity document submission and verification workflows, subject to implementation and configuration.
- 📊 **Booking History and Status Tracking:** Keep track of relevant booking and resource activity.

## 👤 User Roles

### 1. Researcher
- Discover laboratories and scientific equipment.
- View resource information and availability.
- Submit booking requests.
- Choose an available pickup or delivery option.
- Scan QR codes for resource handover and return.
- Track bookings and relevant notifications.

### 2. Provider
- Manage laboratory and equipment listings.
- Update resource information and availability.
- Review, approve, or reject booking requests.
- Display QR codes for direct handovers and returns.
- Receive notifications for verified booking events.
- Monitor resource usage and booking history.

### 3. Delivery Partner
- View assigned deliveries.
- Access relevant pickup and drop-off information.
- Display the appropriate QR code for a delivery handover or return.
- Update permitted delivery statuses.
- Track assigned and completed delivery tasks.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Create an Account:** Register and access the platform using the appropriate role.
2. **Discover Resources:** Researchers explore available laboratories and equipment.
3. **Request a Booking:** Select a resource and submit a booking request.
4. **Provider Approval:** The Provider reviews the request and approves or rejects it.
5. **Handover Verification:** The relevant QR code is displayed by the Provider or Delivery Partner, and the Researcher scans it.
6. **Resource Usage:** The booking becomes active after successful verification.
7. **Return Verification:** The Researcher scans the appropriate return QR code.
8. **Status Updates:** The system records verified events and updates relevant booking statuses and notifications.

*The actual workflow depends on the features configured and implemented in the deployed application.*

## 🔒 Security and Privacy

Resource DNA is intended to handle account information, booking details, and identity verification documents responsibly.

- Protect private user and verification data.
- Enforce role-based access to resources and bookings.
- Validate booking actions and QR credentials on the backend.
- Restrict identity document access to authorized parties.
- Keep credentials and secrets out of source control.
- Use secure storage and appropriate access controls for uploaded documents.
